# ProgressionLESS: Temperature

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Integrates temperature mods into vanilla progression and removes their overlap.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Temperature adds up to 7 research projects depending on your mods. This fork cuts that to at most 3: ceiling fans join Air conditioning, the industrial cooler and heater join VE Temperature's HVAC, and the solar water heater goes back to Dubs Bad Hygiene's Central heating. Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.
