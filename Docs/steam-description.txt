[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3253283971]Progression: Temperature[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Integrates temperature mods into vanilla progression, so heaters, coolers and fans unlock alongside vanilla's air conditioning.
[*]Hides duplicate temperature buildings when several mods overlap, such as Dubs Bad Hygiene's air conditioning with VE Temperature, or Replace Stuff's over-wall coolers and vents.
[*]Gives clearer names to some buildings, such as the standing heater, the solar water heater and the geothermal water heater.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Temperature adds up to [b]7 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork cuts that to [b]at most 3[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Gas heater[/b] and [b]Gas cooler[/b] (Industrial, with VE Helixien Gas)
[*][b]Chemfuel heater[/b] (Industrial, with Appliances Expanded and VE Chemfuel)
[/list]
Everything else unlocks with the research it already builds on:
[list]
[*]Ceiling fans: [b]Air conditioning[/b]
[*]Industrial cooler and industrial heater: VE Temperature's HVAC, now called [b]Industrial climate control[/b]
[*]Solar water heater: Dubs Bad Hygiene's [b]Central heating[/b]
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Vanilla Temperature Expanded
[*]Dubs Bad Hygiene
[*]Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded
[*]Appliances Expanded with Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded
[*]Vanilla Factions Expanded - Classical
[*]Replace Stuff and Replace Stuff - Continued
[*]ReBuild: Doors and Corners
[*]Erin's Cottage Collection
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Temperature for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
